Comms & Resources Summary

547 Responses

Positive Highlights: Educators widely praised Assessment Authoring, Readiness Assessments, and the Help Desk support staff. High school and middle school teachers appreciated released items for benchmark practice.

Common Feedback & Suggestions: Teachers requested making practice test scoring instant/viewable for students, shortening the lengthy administration script in the manual, and moving testing windows closer to May to avoid losing post-test instructional focus.

Student Accommodations Summary

302 Responses

Positive Highlights: TIDE accommodation uploads are considered smooth by coordinators. Small group setups, extended time options, and embedded Speech-to-Text (STT) are acknowledged as critical supports for eligible students.

Common Feedback & Suggestions: Strong request to expand bilingual offerings beyond Spanish (e.g. Arabic, Portuguese, Haitian Creole), upgrade robotic Text-to-Speech (TTS) voices to natural AI voices, and allow turning off bilingual forms without a full test reset.

Student General Tools Summary

474 Responses

Positive Highlights: Older students navigate the interface with ease. The ability to hide the test clock, mark questions for review, and use Desmos calculators receives positive feedback.

Common Feedback & Suggestions: Requests for an Undo button in essay text boxes, enabling the Tab key for paragraph indentation, offering multi-color highlighters, and fixing screen scrolling/zoom issues on smaller Chromebook screens.

TA Interface Summary

474 Responses

Positive Highlights: Proctors agree starting sessions, generating session codes, and approving students is intuitive and fast once familiarized with the dashboard.

Common Feedback & Suggestions: Requests for visual/auditory alerts when students approach time limits (30/15 mins left), easier unpause functions for restroom breaks without requiring re-logins, and moving the "Stop Session" button away from the "Refresh" button.
