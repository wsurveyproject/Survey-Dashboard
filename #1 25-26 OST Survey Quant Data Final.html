Executive Takeaways & Strategic Recommendations

1. Promote Hands-on Practice Tools The Practice Test Site (80.5% approval) and TA Practice Site (79.5% approval) are the highest-rated resources. Student familiarity directly correlates with high navigation ease (83.5%).
2. Equip District Leadership Over 50.9% of educators get primary testing information through local administrators and colleagues. Providing BTCs and DTCs with turnkey toolkits ensures consistent information flow.
